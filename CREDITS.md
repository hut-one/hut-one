# Acknowledgments

Hut.one does not invent cryptography, nor does it claim to. This project is built on the shoulders of giants who have dedicated their careers to making the internet more private and secure. 

We gratefully acknowledge the following projects, researchers, and communities:

## The Cryptographic Bedrock
- **[WireGuard](https://www.wireguard.com/)** by Jason A. Donenfeld. The modern, secure, and blazing-fast tunnel protocol that powers our entire network. Hut.one exists because Jason solved the IPsec/OpenVPN complexity nightmare.
- **[Netfilter / nftables](https://netfilter.org/)**: The powerful packet filtering framework in the Linux kernel that allows us to enforce our strict, fail-closed firewall rules.

## Architectural Inspiration
- **[Obscura VPN](https://obscura.net/)**: Their public research and writing on "Two-Party Relays" and bootstrapping cryptographic trust between a client, a relay, and an exit node directly inspired Hut.one's separation model.
- **[Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/)** & **[IVPN](https://www.ivpn.net/)**: The pioneers of multi-hop privacy networks and uncompromising no-logs architectures.

## The Open-Source Pioneers
- **[Algo VPN](https://github.com/trailofbits/algo)** by Trail of Bits: The project that proved automated, self-hosted cloud VPNs were a viable and necessary tool for the privacy community.
- **[wireguard-install](https://github.com/Nyr/wireguard-install)** by Nyr: The community-standard bash script that has helped thousands of users set up their first WireGuard nodes.
